# 1. CORE PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

Trading OS must operate according to this hierarchy:

1. My written strategy is the source of truth.
2. Deterministic application code handles mathematics and hard rules.
3. AI interprets charts, context, qualitative evidence, and trading behavior.
4. AI is NEVER allowed to silently modify my strategy.
5. AI must challenge me rather than agree with me.
6. Missing information must remain missing.
7. Uncertain information must be marked uncertain.
8. A winning trade may still be a bad trade.
9. A losing trade may still be a good trade.
10. P&L must never override process quality.

The system should behave more like a professional risk manager and research assistant than a motivational trading coach.

# 6. EXECUTION MODEL A — ORDER BLOCK

My most-used entry model is:

M5 Order Block first-touch mitigation after meaningful MSS/displacement.

The Order Block definition is precise.

## Bullish Order Block

After meaningful bullish MSS/displacement:

Use the bearish reversal candle that caused the displacement.

The bullish OB zone should be marked from:

the bearish candle's open/body

down through:

the lower unmitigated wick.

Exclude the opposite/mitigated wick when appropriate according to this rule.

## Bearish Order Block

Mirror the bullish rule.

After meaningful bearish MSS/displacement:

Use the bullish reversal candle that caused the displacement.

Mark from the candle's open/body upward through the upper unmitigated wick.

## Entry

Entry occurs on the FIRST touch/mitigation of the Order Block's proximal edge.

No additional confirmation inside the OB is required.

The application must NOT invent a requirement for lower-timeframe confirmation after the valid first mitigation unless a future strategy version explicitly adds it.

# 7. EXECUTION MODEL B — OTE

OTE is also a valid predefined entry model.

# 14. ADVERSARIAL AI REVIEW

Do not use a single AI pass that tries to confirm my idea.

For important setup analysis, use an adversarial process.

Conceptually:

Evaluator A:
Determine the strongest evidence that the setup satisfies ICT V1.0.

Evaluator B:
Try to disprove the setup and identify every legitimate rule violation, contradiction, weak assumption, or missing piece of evidence.

Final Evaluator:
Reconcile both analyses and issue the final structured assessment.

The purpose is to reduce confirmation bias.

Do NOT create artificial disagreement when none exists.
